The Experience

Experience Abu Dhabi from Dubai

Discover the capital of the United Arab Emirates on a private full-day journey from Dubai to Abu Dhabi. The experience begins with a comfortable morning pickup from your Dubai hotel or Airbnb before travelling approximately 1.5 hours to Abu Dhabi. Your first major stop is the spectacular Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, followed by the Corniche, Heritage Village, Emirates Palace, the traditional Dates Market and Ferrari World before returning to Dubai.

Visitor Regulations

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque — Dress Code & Guidelines

The Grand Mosque is an active sacred place of worship. All visitors are required to follow respectful dress regulations.

Dress Code for Women

  • Loose-fitting, ankle-length clothing
  • Sleeves extending fully to the wrists
  • Full hair coverage with a headscarf
  • Non-transparent and non-revealing fabric
  • Please carry your headscarf with you before leaving Dubai

Dress Code for Men

  • Modest clothing covering the legs (long trousers)
  • Shorts are strictly prohibited
  • Shirts with sleeves (no sleeveless vests/tank tops)
  • Non-transparent clothing
  • Respectful attire suitable for a religious sanctuary

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Abu Dhabi tour from Dubai?

The complete private tour lasts approximately 10 hours, including the journey between Dubai and Abu Dhabi (approx. 1.5 hours each way).

Do we get inside Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque?

Yes. The tour includes inside access to explore the main prayer hall, majestic chandeliers, and world-record hand-knotted carpet.

Can women wear trousers to the mosque?

Yes, provided they are loose-fitting and cover the ankles. Women must also have their arms covered to the wrists and hair completely covered with a headscarf.

Is lunch included?

Lunch is not included. Your driver-guide can suggest authentic local restaurants or international dining spots along the Corniche or Marina Mall depending on your preference.
