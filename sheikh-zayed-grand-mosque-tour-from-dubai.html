Important Guidelines

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Dress Code

We strongly recommend wearing appropriate clothing before leaving Dubai so you are prepared for mosque entry.

Dress Code for Women

  • Long, loose-fitting clothing covering ankles
  • Sleeves extending fully to the wrists
  • A headscarf covering the hair completely
  • Non-transparent, non-tight and non-revealing clothing
  • Women must carry a headscarf

Dress Code for Men

  • Long, modest trousers covering the legs
  • Shorts are strictly not allowed
  • Shirts with sleeves (no sleeveless vests)
  • Non-transparent and respectful clothing

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the transfer experience from Dubai?

The total experience is approximately 5 hours, including the round-trip journey between Dubai and Abu Dhabi and your independent mosque visit.

Does the driver come inside the mosque?

No. The driver provides transportation and assists with registration and entry access, but waits at the designated vehicle area while you explore inside at your own pace.

What if we want to see more of Abu Dhabi?

If you would like to visit multiple Abu Dhabi attractions (Corniche, Heritage Village, Dates Market, Ferrari World) with a driver-cum-tour-guide, we recommend our 10-hour Abu Dhabi Sightseeing Tour from Dubai instead.
