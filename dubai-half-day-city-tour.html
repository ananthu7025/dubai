Dubai Half-Day Tour Itinerary

Carefully coordinated 4-hour route linking historic heritage and modern engineering marvels.

1

Dubai Creek & Traditional Abra Crossing

Begin your tour in the historic heart of Dubai with a traditional Abra crossing across Dubai Creek. Experience one of the city’s oldest and most characteristic ways of travelling between the historic districts of Bur Dubai and Deira.

Included in tour price • Pier ticket handled by your guide
2

Gold Souk & Spice Souk Walking Discovery

Explore two of the most famous traditional covered markets in Old Dubai. Stroll through fragrant spice alleys rich with saffron, frankincense, and dates, then admire the record-breaking jewelry displays at the Gold Souk.

Zero shopping pressure • Honest local merchant tips
3

Dubai Frame Architecture Viewpoint

Cruise into Modern Dubai for a prime exterior photo stop at the Dubai Frame in Zabeel Park. Marvel at the 150-meter golden structure designed to frame Old Dubai to the north and the Downtown skyline to the south.

Outside photo stop with optimal sunlight angles
4

Museum of the Future

Stop alongside the world's most beautiful building along Sheikh Zayed Road. Admire the torus design adorned with Arabic calligraphy poetry by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum.

Dedicated photo spot reserved for our private vehicles
5

Palm Jumeirah & Atlantis Scenic Drive

Enjoy a panoramic drive down the trunk and crescent of the world-famous Palm Jumeirah man-made archipelago. Gaze out at luxury beachfront villas, the Arabian Gulf horizon, and the regal Atlantis The Royal resort.

Panoramic private cruise with chilled water
6

Burj Al Arab Iconic Coastal Stop

Arrive at the private viewpoint overlooking the 7-star sail-shaped Burj Al Arab hotel. Capture postcards alongside pristine white sand and azure coastal waters before the afternoon heat sets in.

Driver guides you to the finest crowd-free angles
7

Chauffeured Return to Hotel or Airbnb

Relax in your chilled private vehicle as your chauffeur drives you smoothly back to your accommodation or drops you at Dubai Mall / Downtown if you wish to continue exploring independently.

Door-to-door comfort • Flexible drop-off options

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your private Dubai half-day tour.

What is included in the Dubai half-day city tour?

The tour includes a private air-conditioned vehicle, professional driver-cum-guide, Dubai hotel/Airbnb pickup and drop-off, bottled water and a traditional Abra crossing across Dubai Creek.

Is this a private Dubai city tour?

Yes. This is a private Dubai sightseeing tour exclusively for your group. You will not be combined with other guests.

How long is the Dubai half-day sightseeing tour?

The tour is approximately 4 hours, although the actual duration can vary depending on traffic and time spent at the souks.

Do you offer hotel and Airbnb pickup?

Yes. Pickup and drop-off are available from hotels and Airbnb accommodations around Dubai.

Is the Abra ride included?

Yes. The traditional Dubai Creek Abra crossing is included in the tour price.

Are Dubai Frame and Museum of the Future tickets included?

No. Both are outside photo stops on this itinerary. Entrance tickets are not included.

Can we shop at the Gold Souk and Spice Souk?

Yes. Guests can explore both souks and spend some time shopping if they wish. Shopping is optional.

Is food included?

No. Food is not included. Bottled drinking water is provided throughout the tour.

Can I book the tour for two people?

Yes. The tour is private regardless of group size. Pricing is based on the complete private vehicle/tour, not per person.

Can I request a luxury vehicle?

Yes. VIP and luxury vehicle upgrades may be available depending on your preferred vehicle and availability.
