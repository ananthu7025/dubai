Your Journey

Your Old Dubai Walking Tour Itinerary

A relaxed combination of air-conditioned transfers, atmospheric walking, and tasty stops.

1

Private Hotel or Airbnb Pickup

Your private driver meets you in your hotel lobby or outside your Airbnb in an air-conditioned SUV/van. Smooth, private transit straight to Dubai Creek with chilled water provided.

Convenient morning pickup • Flexible starting time
2

Al Seef Waterfront Promenade

Begin your walking discovery at Al Seef, where reconstructed 1950s coral-stone architecture lines the water’s edge. Enjoy views of historic dhow boats, artisanal workshops, and quiet morning water breezes.

Waterfront stroll • Authentic photo spots
3

Al Fahidi Historical District (Bastakiya)

Step through Dubai's oldest standing residential quarter. Wander past authentic wind towers (barjeel) that cooled homes over a century ago, discover hidden art courtyards, and hear how pearl-diving families lived.

Historic architecture • Shaded lanes
4

Local Street Food & Flavour Tastings

Savor authentic Dubai flavors included in your tour: freshly grilled spiced shawarma, cooling hibiscus juice or fragrant mint tea, premium stuffed dates, and handcrafted camel-milk chocolates.

All 4 food & drink tastings included • Vegetarian options
5

Traditional Abra Creek Crossing

Board an authentic wooden Abra boat for a scenic trip across Dubai Creek to Deira. Feel the sea breeze and observe the bustling maritime trade that has defined Dubai since the early 1900s.

Abra crossing tickets included & pre-arranged
6

Spice Souk & Gold Souk Sensory Exploration

Immerse yourself in vibrant covered markets. Test your senses at the Spice Souk with saffron, cardamom and rosebuds, then admire the glittering windows and world-record rings in the legendary Gold Souk.

Zero merchant pressure • Your guide advises on fair prices
7

Private Chauffeured Drop-Off

Once you finish your explorations and optional shopping, your driver meets you curbside in your cooled vehicle and returns you safely to your hotel or next Dubai destination.

Total duration ~4 hours • Door-to-door comfort

Walking Guidelines

A Relaxed Walking Experience

Walking is an important part of discovering Old Dubai, but this is not a continuous 1-hour walking tour. Guests can expect approximately 1 hour of walking in total, spread across different sections of the experience with breaks between stops.

What to Bring & Wear

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • Carrying an umbrella for sun protection
  • Light, breathable clothing suitable for the weather
  • Camera or smartphone for authentic photos

What Makes This Tour Special?

  • Authentic Old Dubai: Beyond the high-rises and skyscrapers.
  • Walk Through History: 19th-century wind towers and coral-stone lanes.
  • Local Flavours: Shawarma, tea/juice, dates & chocolate tastings.
  • Traditional Abra Ride: Historic Creek crossing by water.
  • Private Guide: Personalised attention without rush.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Old Dubai Walking Tour?

The complete experience lasts approximately 4 hours, including private hotel pickup and drop-off.

How much walking is involved?

Guests can expect approximately 1 hour of walking in total, spread across different parts of the tour. It is not a continuous 1-hour walk.

What time does the tour start?

Pickup is around 8:00 AM from your Dubai hotel or Airbnb to enjoy the pleasant morning weather in the historical district.

What food tastings are included?

The tour includes authentic shawarma, Arabic tea or a hibiscus juice shot, dates tasting and chocolate tasting.

Can we shop and bargain at the souks?

Yes. You will have free time to explore and shop at the Spice Souk and Gold Souk. Guests can bargain directly with sellers where appropriate.
