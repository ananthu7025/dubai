Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about our bespoke private sightseeing packages.

What makes a private tour better than a group tour?

No waiting for other guests, no crowded buses, and no fixed group itinerary. You move at your own pace with a dedicated private vehicle, hotel pickup, and a licensed local guide catering to your interests.

Do you offer door-to-door hotel pickup and drop-off?

Yes. All our private tours include direct door-to-door pickup and drop-off from any hotel, residence, or airport terminal across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Can we customize the stops and duration of our itinerary?

Absolutely. Your itinerary is 100% personalised. You can spend more time at the landmarks you love, skip unwanted stops, or add experiences like Burj Khalifa or private yacht cruises.

How does WhatsApp support work before and during the tour?

Our concierge team coordinates directly with you on WhatsApp for instant confirmation, live driver tracking, itinerary adjustments, and real-time support throughout your day.
