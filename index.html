Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about our premium tour packages

Do you provide airport pickups for transit layovers?

Yes. We coordinate direct meets at Dubai International (DXB) or Abu Dhabi (AUH) terminals, tracking your flight status in real-time to adjust for any delays.

What is the dress code for visiting the Grand Mosque?

All visitors must dress modestly. Women require loose, long clothing covering wrists and ankles, and a headscarf. Men require long trousers and sleeved shirts.

Are admission tickets included in the tour packages?

Our all-inclusive private tours secure all fast-track entry passes (e.g. Dubai Frame, Abra crossings) beforehand, so you bypass ticket counter lines entirely.

Can we customize the route during the tour?

Absolutely. Because our tours are privately hosted, you can consult with your driver-guide to adjust stops, spend more time at photo spots, or skip segments.
