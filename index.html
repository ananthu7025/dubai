Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about our bespoke private sightseeing packages.

What makes a private tour better than a group tour?

No waiting for other guests, no crowded buses, and no fixed group itinerary. You move at your own pace with a dedicated private vehicle, hotel pickup, and a licensed local guide catering to your interests.

Do you offer door-to-door hotel pickup and drop-off?

Yes. All our private tours include direct door-to-door pickup and drop-off from any hotel, residence, or airport terminal across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Will there be a separate tour guide and driver?

The arrangement depends on the size of your group. For groups of up to 6 guests, we provide a driver-cum-tour guide who is also a licensed Dubai tour guide. For larger groups, a separate licensed tour guide may be provided to ensure greater comfort and personalized attention.

Will the tour guide stay with us throughout the tour?

Yes. Our licensed tour guides accompany you throughout the sightseeing experience. They will park the vehicle and join you at the attractions, provide information and assistance, and are also happy to help with photographs during your tour.

Do you provide bottled water in the vehicle?

Yes. Complimentary bottled water is provided in the vehicle for our guests throughout the tour.

What happens if I need more time than the scheduled tour duration?

If you would like to extend your tour beyond the allocated duration, additional hours can be arranged at an applicable hourly rate. Your guide will inform you of the additional charges, and the final amount will be calculated based on the actual extra time used.

What happens if I need to cancel my tour the day before?

Our cancellation policy requires cancellations to be made at least 48 hours before the scheduled tour to qualify for a 100% refund, less any applicable bank or payment processing charges. Cancellations made within 48 hours may be subject to our cancellation terms.

How does WhatsApp support work before and during the tour?

Our concierge team coordinates directly with you on WhatsApp for instant confirmation, live driver tracking, itinerary adjustments, and real-time support throughout your day.
