Step-by-Step

How the Jet Ski Experience Works

1

Private Hotel / Airbnb Pickup

Your private driver collects you from your Dubai hotel or Airbnb in a cooled vehicle and escorts you directly to the beach water sports marina.

Door-to-door private transport
2

Locker Room, Changing & Gear Up

Change into your swimwear in air-conditioned facilities. Store all valuables in complimentary private security lockers and fit your high-buoyancy life vest.

Lockers and changing rooms included
3

Instructor Briefing & Throttle Training

Your certified marine instructor walks you through emergency shutoffs, throttle control, and harbor navigation. No prior jet ski license or experience needed.

Beginner-friendly instruction
4

Open Water Acceleration

Power past the harbor breakwater into open Arabian Gulf waters. Accelerate across waves on high-performance Yamaha Waverunners with your partner.

Lead guide ensures safe perimeter
5

Burj Al Arab Viewpoint & Photo Shoot

Cut the engines at the calm ocean photo bay directly beside the Burj Al Arab. Your lead instructor takes high-resolution action photos and videos of you and your partner on your phones.

Free photo assistance by your guide
6

Marina Return, Freshwater Shower & Chauffeur Transfer

Return to the marina pontoon, rinse off in freshwater beach showers, and enjoy cold bottled water in your waiting private vehicle for a smooth drive home.

Full round-trip service
